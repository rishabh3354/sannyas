## Full description (4000 max)

```
Sannyas is a free audio player for Osho's recorded discourses, built for people who actually listen to them — long talks, often at night, often offline.

351 discourse series. 5,518 talks. Around 8,257 hours of listening, in both Hindi and English.

LISTEN THE WAY YOU ACTUALLY LISTEN

Osho's talks usually run over an hour, so Sannyas is built around that rather than around three-minute songs:

• Sleep timer — set a duration, or let playback stop at the end of the current talk
• Resume exactly where you stopped, on every talk, even after a restart
• Playback speed control
• Skip silence
• Background playback that keeps going with the screen off
• Lock screen and notification controls
• Bluetooth, wired headsets and media buttons, including pause when you unplug

LISTEN WITHOUT A CONNECTION

Download a single talk or an entire series for offline listening. Downloads continue in the background and survive a restart. Downloaded audio is kept separate from the streaming cache, so clearing one never removes the other.

HINDI AND ENGLISH

176 Hindi series and 175 English series — Tao Upanishad, Krishna, Prashnottar, Es Dhammo Sanantano, and many more. Choose which language you want to browse, and change it any time.

FIND WHAT YOU WANT

• Search across series, talks and categories
• Browse 55 categories — Zen, Buddha, Krishna, Sufism, Tao, Kabir, Yoga, Western Mystics and more
• Radio, for when you would rather not choose
• Favourites, playlists, bookmarks and listening history

IN THE CAR

Full Android Auto support. Browse the whole catalogue from the car screen, or ask your assistant to play a series.

MADE TO BE PLEASANT

Dark and light themes with Material You dynamic colour, and player colours drawn from each series' artwork. Designed to work properly with screen readers, with correct touch targets and text that scales with your system font size.

NO ACCOUNT. NO ADS. NO TRACKING.

There is no sign-up, no subscription, and no advertising. Sannyas has no server of its own and contains no analytics or tracking code of any kind. Your favourites, history and settings stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall.

Audio is streamed on demand from oshoworld.com, where it is offered freely.

ABOUT THIS APP

Sannyas is an independent player. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to oshoworld.com or Osho International Foundation. Audio is streamed directly from the source rather than rehosted, and no discourse text is redistributed. References to Osho identify whose discourses the app plays; they are not a claim of affiliation.

Website: https://warlordsoftwares.gitlab.io/sannyas/
Privacy policy: https://warlordsoftwares.gitlab.io/sannyas/privacy-policy.html
```
